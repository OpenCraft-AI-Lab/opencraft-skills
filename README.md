# OpenCraft Skills

Reusable production skills distilled from OpenCraft AI Lab projects.

## Skills

| Skill | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| [AI video director](skills/ai-video-director/SKILL.md) | Planning, generating, editing, and checking a multi-shot AI product ad or short drama. |

Copy the `skills/ai-video-director/` folder into the skills directory used by your agent. Read its `SKILL.md` first; it links to the workflow and the relevant example. The skill is based on EP11's Meta Muse productions, but its production decisions can be used with other image and video generators after checking their current capabilities.

| Form | EP11 example | What it teaches |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product ad | [OpenCraft perfume](skills/ai-video-director/references/examples/perfume-ad.md) | Product lock, one lighting progression, continuity at cut points, music and brand timing |
| AI short drama | [Tiny Courier](skills/ai-video-director/references/examples/tiny-courier.md) | Ending-first visual gag, miniature scale and action-window editing |
| AI short drama | [Cloud Nine](skills/ai-video-director/references/examples/cloud-nine.md) | Nine story jobs, dialogue diagnosis and picture repairs around continuous music |

Source video, private workspace screenshots, generated audio, and project files are not included. Case details are bounded by the recorded EP11 production state, rather than claims about current Muse features or prices.
